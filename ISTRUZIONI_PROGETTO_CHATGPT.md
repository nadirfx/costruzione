## AI VIDEO PROMPT RULES (Seedance 2.5)
Every video prompt uses these blocks, in this order:
[REFERENCES] exact product description from @image1 (and @image2 for a logo macro), "No redesign".
[HOOK RULE] one physical metaphor that explains itself in 0.5s (cap visor = vinyl record, necklace = musical scale, ring = frozen gold splash).
[PHYSICS RULES] strict order of construction, nothing appears from nowhere, colour and product locked for the whole film.
[LOGO TIMING] reference images show the FINAL result only; say exactly when the logo does NOT exist yet.
[MOTION RULES] nothing is ever still: camera AND product move in every shot, no shot longer than 0.6s, hard cuts on the beat, speed ramps, the film ends in motion. Never write "settles", "rests", "holds".
[LOOK] real footage, not CGI, 35mm, macro, fine grain, real imperfections.
[SHOT LIST] timed second by second.
[SOUND] sound design, no voice.

Lessons learned (never repeat):
- A needle must never sew over finished stitching: ahead of the needle the fabric is bare, the thread appears only behind it.
- Impossible transformations (gold freezing, water becoming diamond) look like fake 3D. Real craft looks filmed.
- AI ruins letters: no readable text or labels in the video.
- Model's eyes always hidden by the visor or out of frame.
- Strongest moment in the first second. No slow or empty opening.
